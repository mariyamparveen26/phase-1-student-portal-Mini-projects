My Favorite Dish

Ice cream is my favorite

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Fresh Cream
  • Sugar
  • Cocoa Powder
  • Vanilla essence
  • Melted Chocolate

Instructions

  1. Mix the ingredients: Take milk, fresh cream, and sugar in a bowl. Mix them well until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Add cocoa powder: Add cocoa powder and mix until there are no lumps.
  3. Add vanilla essence: Add a few drops of vanilla essence for extra flavor.
  4. Add melted chocolate: Pour in the melted chocolate and mix everything until smooth.
  5. Freeze the mixture: Pour the mixture into a container and cover it with a lid.
  6. Stir the mixture: Freeze for about 2 hours, then stir it well to make the ice cream smoother.
  7. Freeze again: Put it back in the freezer for another 4–6 hours, or until it becomes firm.
  8. Serve and enjoy: Scoop the chocolate ice cream into a bowl and enjoy your homemade dessert!

Description of Dish

Ice cream is my favorite dish. I love chocolate ice cream because it is sweet, creamy, and delicious.
It is made with milk, fresh cream, sugar, cocoa powder, vanilla essence, and melted chocolate.
It has a rich chocolate flavor and a smooth texture. I enjoy eating ice cream, especially during hot weather.
It makes me feel happy and refreshed.
